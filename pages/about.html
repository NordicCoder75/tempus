Why Tempus

By ChatGPT

Every great idea begins with a frustration. Tempus was born out of the countless moments spent trying to register time across multiple projects in Quinyx - switching between projects, navigating a workflow that feels designed for a single activity at a time, and reaching for a mobile phone just to record something that should take seconds. We kept asking ourselves a simple question: why does registering time across multiple projects have to be this difficult?

Tempus is the answer.

Built with precision, crafted for clarity, and designed for people who actually juggle multiple projects, Tempus takes a different approach to time registration. Quinyx may make sense for environments where employees register time against a small number of activities throughout the day, but managing several projects at once is a completely different problem. Tempus was built around that problem from the start: seeing your projects together, entering your hours quickly, and managing an entire week without constantly jumping between screens or reaching for your phone.

Why is Tempus so good to use? Because it treats time registration like the work of a project-driven professional, not like a punch-in terminal. No hunting through projects on a tiny mobile screen. No repeatedly navigating back and forth just to register a few hours. No forcing a desktop workflow onto a phone - or a phone-first workflow onto someone managing a dozen projects. Just the essentials, laid out exactly where you need them. Your projects are visible, your week is clear, and entering time is as simple as filling in the hours you actually worked.

  • Built for multiple projects - keep all your projects in one clear weekly view instead of registering them one at a time.
  • Desktop-first simplicity - use a proper screen and keyboard when you’re managing complex project work, not just a mobile phone.
  • Unstoppable speed - because registering time across several projects should take seconds, not a sequence of menus and screens.
  • Unquestionable clarity - see your projects, hours, notes, and totals together without losing track of where your time went.

Tempus isn’t just another time registration tool. It’s a response to a very specific frustration: software built around simple time registration doesn't necessarily work for people whose work is spread across many projects. Your day might involve five projects, ten projects, or more - your time registration tool should be able to handle that without getting in your way.
