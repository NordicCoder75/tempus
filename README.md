# Tempus

**Tempus** is a lightweight, browser-based time registration tool built for people who use Quinyx and work across multiple projects.

## Why Tempus?

Tempus started with a simple frustration: **registering time across multiple projects in Quinyx can be unnecessarily cumbersome.**

When your work is spread across several projects, switching between projects and screens just to register a few hours quickly becomes tedious. Tempus was created as a simpler alternative, with a focus on seeing your projects together and registering time for an entire week in one place.

Instead of treating time registration like a punch-in system, Tempus is designed around project-based work: **see your projects, enter your hours, add notes, and keep track of your weekly totals - all from one clear view.**

---

## ✨ Features

* ⚡ **Fast time entry** - quickly register hours and notes
* 📅 **Weekly overview** - see your projects and registered time in one place
* 📊 **Yearly reports** - see the total hours spent on each project across an entire year
* 📂 **Multiple projects** - manage several projects without constantly switching screens
* 🔄 **Easy week navigation** - switch between weeks instantly
* ➕ **Simple row creation** - add new projects or entries when needed
* 🧮 **Automatic totals** - keep track of your hours without manual calculations
* 💾 **Local storage** - your data is stored directly in the browser
* 🎯 **Distraction-free interface** - focused on the essentials

> **Your work is complicated enough. Registering your time shouldn't be.**
